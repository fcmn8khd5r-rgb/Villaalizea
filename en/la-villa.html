The villa

Room by room

Three hundred and twenty square metres on one level, arranged around the living room. No room is crossed to reach another: every bedroom has its own door onto the terrace, and eight people can live here without ever getting in each other's way.

01

The living room

Sixty square metres without a single partition, under a ceiling that climbs to the ridge beam. The glass doors slide away into the wall, and the living room and the terrace stop being two rooms.

  • 645 sq ft
  • Cathedral ceiling
  • Pocket glass doors
  • Ceiling fans

02

The kitchen

Four metres of polished concrete worktop, a long table built from reclaimed wood, and everything you need to skip the restaurant: induction hob, oven, dishwasher, coffee machine and wine fridge.

  • Seats 10
  • Induction hob
  • Dishwasher
  • Wine fridge

03

The bedrooms

Four bedrooms, all on one level, each with air conditioning and mosquito screens. Three have a double bed; the fourth has twin beds that separate — the one families always take.

  • 4 bedrooms
  • Sleeps 8
  • Air conditioning
  • Mosquito screens

04

The bathrooms

Three bathrooms in lime plaster, with walk-in showers and bronze fittings. The hot water is solar — there is enough all day, and you never think about it.

  • 3 bathrooms
  • Walk-in showers
  • Solar hot water
  • Linen provided

05

The terrace and the pool

A pergola, a day bed that falls into shade through the afternoon, and a twelve-metre lap pool edged in smooth concrete. Beyond it, the garden of sand and coconut palms.

  • 12 m lap pool
  • Pergola
  • Plancha grill
  • Outdoor shower

Services
